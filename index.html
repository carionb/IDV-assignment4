Subzone Demographics: Age Patterns in Singapore

This map illustrates the age structure of Singapore’s subzones by comparing each area’s population composition to the national average.

Instead of simply showing the dominant age group (which is typically the working-age population), the visualization highlights subzones where specific age groups: Children (0–14), Working Age (15–64), or Seniors (65+) are over-represented.

This approach reveals subzones with distinctive demographic profiles, offering insights for urban planning, community programming, and age-targeted services.
